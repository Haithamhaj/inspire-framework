# INSPIRE SEO Strategy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

## Goal

Position INSPIRE as a specialist product for people who want AI tools to understand their work style and generate better, copy-ready instructions for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and similar assistants.

## Market Context

The GCC and Saudi market is a strong fit because workplace and consumer AI adoption is high and still expanding. The immediate SEO opportunity is not broad "AI news"; it is practical, intent-driven search around prompts, prompt engineering, AI productivity, ChatGPT instructions, and using AI at work.

## Recommended Landing Positioning

Use a clear product category:

> AI Operating Profile and Prompt Instructions

Arabic support phrase:

> ملف تشغيل وتعليمات مخصصة للذكاء الاصطناعي

Avoid making the product sound like a generic AI chatbot, prompt list, course, or consulting service. INSPIRE is a digital assessment that produces a personalized operating profile and copy-ready instructions.

## Content Roadmap

Create a small, high-quality content hub after Lemon review:

1. `/guides/how-to-write-better-prompts`
   - Target: "how to write better prompts", "كيف أكتب برومبت"
   - Purpose: broad educational entry point.

2. `/guides/chatgpt-custom-instructions`
   - Target: "ChatGPT custom instructions", "تعليمات ChatGPT"
   - Purpose: direct bridge to INSPIRE output.

3. `/guides/prompt-engineering-for-work`
   - Target: "prompt engineering for work", "استخدام الذكاء الاصطناعي في العمل"
   - Purpose: GCC/Saudi workplace productivity angle.

4. `/guides/ai-operating-profile`
   - Target: "AI operating profile"
   - Purpose: define and own the category.

5. `/guides/arabic-ai-prompts`
   - Target: Arabic prompt terms and bilingual AI usage.
   - Purpose: differentiate for GCC/Middle East users.
