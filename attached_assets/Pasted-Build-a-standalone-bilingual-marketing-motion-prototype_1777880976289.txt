Build a standalone bilingual marketing motion prototype for INSPIRE.

Goal:
Create a 15–18 second looping explainer video that shows:
1) the user writes a natural request,
2) INSPIRE builds an operating profile,
3) the same request becomes clearer for AI because it now includes better operating context.

Important:
- Do not imply magical AI behavior.
- Do not imply AI “knows the person” in a human sense.
- The message is: INSPIRE adds structured operating context to the request.

Do not modify production pages if this is a prototype.
Use static mock content only.

Visual style:
- premium dark UI
- glass cards
- subtle rose/orange/violet glow
- modern SaaS feel
- smooth elegant motion
- bilingual support:
  - Arabic when lang=ar
  - English when lang=en

Video structure:

SCENE 1 — 0s to 5s
Show a user typing a natural prompt in an AI chat interface.
Prompt example:
AR: "ساعدني أرتب مشروعي"
EN: "Help me organize my project"

A general response begins to appear.
The message is not “bad prompt”, but “missing operating context”.

On-screen text:
AR: "طلب واضح… لكن بدون ملف تشغيل"
EN: "A clear request… but without an operating profile"

SCENE 2 — 5s to 11s
Show a glowing central INSPIRE panel.
Text:
AR: "INSPIRE يبني ملف تشغيل للـ AI"
EN: "INSPIRE builds an operating profile for AI"

Animate 5 cards emerging one by one from the INSPIRE panel.
Each card should:
- appear with fade/slide
- cycle through 3 short values
- settle on one final value

Card 1:
AR title: "الدور"
EN title: "Role"
AR rotating values: "خبير", "كوتش", "مساعد"
EN rotating values: "Expert", "Coach", "Assistant"
Final:
AR: "خبير"
EN: "Expert"
Helper:
AR: "يحدد زاوية تعامل AI مع الطلب"
EN: "Defines how AI should approach the request"

EN: "Direct & Analytical"
Helper:
AR: "يشكل نبرة الرد وطريقته"
EN: "Shapes the tone and style of the response"

Card 3:

EN rotating values: "Steps", "Summary", "Plan"

AR: "استراتيجية + خطوة بخطوة"

Show the same AI interface again.
